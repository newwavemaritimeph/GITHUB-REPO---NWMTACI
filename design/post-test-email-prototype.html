How to Take the Post-Test and Evaluation

  1. Post-test: click Take the Post-Test. It opens on your phone or computer; no login is needed. Answer all 10 questions, then click Submit Answers. Your score shows right away. If it is below 70%, click Retake the Test and try again.
  2. Evaluation: click Evaluate the Training. Tick Confirm for attendance, rate each item from Excellent to Poor, write your comments and suggestions, then click Submit Evaluation.
  3. Lost this email? Go to newwavemaritimemtaci.com/registration-search, enter your SRN and NWMTACI No. (NWMTACI-0000012), then click Take Post-Test or Evaluate Training.
  4. Need help? Call or text 0948 847 6530, or email newwavemaritime@gmail.com.

Your certificate is released once your fee is settled, your post-test is passed and your evaluation is submitted.
